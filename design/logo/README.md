# Redline logo

**The mark (revision 2):** a rendered tachometer at the instant its needle is pinned against the red band. The needle is the agent at full throttle; the red band is the policy it cannot cross. Built as a crafted vector instrument (brushed bezel, graphite face, engraved ticks, glass highlight, a warm glow only at the contact point), so it stays sharp at 16px and the circle crop that pump.fun and ClawPump apply lands on the bezel.

Revision 1 (a flat two-shape needle and rule) was rejected at Checkpoint A: too thin, read as clip-art next to ClawPump's illustrated token pictures. Photoreal image-model routes were tried and rejected: the free route turns every gauge into a wristwatch or compass with fake numerals and a watermark (renders/gallery-r2.png, gallery-r3.png); the Gemini image model has zero free-tier quota on this key.

**The wordmark:** lowercase "redline" in Barlow Semi Condensed SemiBold (a road-sign grotesk, OFL licensed), with the red rule sitting on the ascenders so the d and the l rise to the line and stop. Letters are outlined to paths; no font is needed to render it.

**Palette:** red `#E03A2F`, graphite `#131417`, off-white `#F4F3EF`. No gradients, no second colour.
