# Art direction: Redline

## The lazy first ideas, named and forbidden
1. A speedometer / full gauge dial. It is the category cliché for "limit" and reads as an analytics widget at small sizes.
2. A red octagon stop sign, a shield, a padlock. Security clichés; also say "blocked", not "held under a line".
3. A crab claw or pincer. ClawPump's own motif; a hundred entrants will use it.
4. A candlestick or line chart with a red line through it. Trading-dashboard cliché.

## Signature element
A needle stopped flush against a line. One neutral needle rising from bottom-left toward top-right at speed, its tip landing exactly on a single horizontal red rule and going no further. Two shapes. The needle is the agent at full throttle; the red rule is the policy. Nothing is broken, nothing is blocked; it is held.

The same idea carries the wordmark: lowercase "redline" set in a road-sign grotesk, with a red rule sitting at ascender height so the d and the l rise to the line and stop.

## Material
Flat, matte, printed. No glass, no depth, no glow. A mark that could be stencilled on an engine cover.

## Composition
Centred inside the middle 70% of the tile (circle-crop safe). Generous negative space. High-contrast silhouette: light needle on graphite, or graphite needle on off-white. The red rule is the only colour.

## Palette
Accent `#E03A2F` only. Graphite `#131417`. Off-white `#F4F3EF`.

## Forbidden list (hard)
No text, letters or numbers in the mark. No gradients. No glow, sparkles, neon. No second colour. No gauge dial, no stop sign, no shield, no padlock, no claw, no chart, no robot, no hexagon. No 3D.

## Dual legibility
At 16px: a slanted bar under a red bar. At 400px: the needle's taper and the exact flush contact with the rule reward a closer look.

## Route
Vector (SVG). The 16px floor and the flat material make raster generation the wrong tool.

# Revision 2 (2026-09-18, after Checkpoint A with the user)

The flat two-shape mark was rejected by the user ("does not feel right") and by honest review: thin stroke plus a blob, reads as a clip-art gauge fragment next to ClawPump's illustrated token pictures. Route changes to **raster, image model**, with the vector wordmark kept.

## Signature element, revised
A tight crop of a real tachometer face at the moment the needle is pinned against the red band. The dial is the circle ClawPump crops to. The needle is bright and slender, machined, its tip hard against the start of the red zone. The red band is the only colour. A faint warm glow only where needle meets red. Numerals are optional but never legible text; tick marks fine.

## Material
Photoreal instrument craft: matte graphite dial, brushed steel or dark anodised bezel, glass with one soft reflection, real depth and shadow. Not cartoon, not neon, not glass-blob.

## Composition
Dial centred, filling about 80% of the square so the circle crop lands on the bezel. Needle at roughly 1 o'clock. Generous dark field outside the bezel.

## Palette
Signal red #E03A2F band. Graphite, steel, off-white needle. No green, no blue, no rainbow.

## Forbidden (hard negatives)
No text, letters, numbers as words, brand names, watermarks. No crab or claw. No car, no dashboard, no steering wheel, no speed lines. No neon, no sparkles, no lens flare, no rainbow. No blue or green accents. No cartoon or flat vector. No second dial.

## Dual legibility
32px: a dark disc with a bright needle touching a red arc. 400px: bezel machining, glass reflection, the exact contact point.
